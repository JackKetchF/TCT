# Coherent Time Theory (TCT)

This repository contains the **core of Coherent Time Theory (TCT)** —  
a minimal, falsifiable framework proposing a coherence-based description of time.

TCT introduces a scalar coherence field 𝓒(x,t) whose dynamics are consistent
with known physical limits and lead to a single, concrete experimental prediction.
The theory is intentionally narrow in scope and designed to be testable or falsified.

---

## What This Theory Is

- A **minimal physical hypothesis** about the structure of time
- A **dynamical field model**, not a philosophical interpretation
- A framework **consistent with General Relativity in tested regimes**
- A theory with **one primary, falsifiable experimental prediction**

---

## What This Theory Is Not

- Not a theory of consciousness, metaphysics, or free will  
- Not a replacement for General Relativity or Quantum Mechanics  
- Not an attempt at unification  
- Not an unfalsifiable or purely interpretive model  

Any speculative extensions are explicitly excluded from the core.

## Repository Structure

### Core Documents

- **FORMULAS.md**

- **EXPERIMENT.md**  
  Minimal experimental test

- **LIMITATIONS.md**  
  Known limitations, open problems, and scope boundaries  
  Explicitly states where the theory may fail

- **FAQ.md**  
  Reviewer-oriented clarifications  
  Addresses likely objections and misunderstandings

## Experimental Status

TCT makes **one primary prediction**:

> The existence of low-frequency correlated temporal fluctuations  
> between nominally independent precision clocks.

If this correlation is not observed below the stated sensitivity,
**TCT v1.0 is falsified without modification**.

---

## Scientific Status

- TCT is a **testable hypothesis**
- It is **not established** and makes no claim of acceptance
- Its validity depends entirely on experimental outcome

Null results are considered informative and decisive.
