AXIOMS

Axiom 1 (Primacy of Coherence)
Reality is fundamentally structured by coherence.
All stable phenomena arise from sustained coherent relations.

Axiom 2 (Time as a Coherence Process)
Time is not a fundamental dimension but an emergent process
resulting from the evolution of coherence within systems.

Axiom 3 (Information–Structure Equivalence)
Information and structure are equivalent descriptions
of coherent states at different levels of abstraction.

Axiom 4 (Observer Participation)
Observation is an active interaction that modifies
the coherence of the observed system.

Axiom 5 (Scale Invariance of Coherence)
The same principles of coherence apply across all scales,
from quantum systems to cosmological structures.

Axiom 6 (Instability and Emergence)
Loss or reconfiguration of coherence leads to phase transitions,
through which new structures and laws may emerge.

Axiom 7 (Causality as Ordered Coherence)
Causality is not fundamental but arises from ordered
sequences of coherent states.

Axiom 8 (Reality as a Self-Consistent System)
Reality evolves to maintain global self-consistency
through local adjustments of coherence.
