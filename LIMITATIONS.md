# LIMITATIONS.md — Limitations & Open Problems (TCT)
## Transparency Statement (v1.0)

This document enumerates the **known limitations**, unresolved questions, and scope boundaries
of the Theory of Coherent Time (TCT). These are not weaknesses to be hidden, but constraints
to be tested.

---

## 1. Lack of Direct Measurement of 𝓒

The coherence field 𝓒 is not directly observable.
All access to 𝓒 is indirect, via correlated temporal fluctuations.

**Implication:**
- Model validation relies on statistical inference, not point measurements.
- Misinterpretation of noise sources is a primary risk.

---

## 2. Model Dependence of the Potential V(𝓒)

The self-interaction potential V(𝓒) is not uniquely fixed in v1.0.

**Implication:**
- Different functional forms may fit data equally well.
- Parameter degeneracy must be controlled by minimality and falsification.

---

## 3. Separation from Known Noise Sources

Low-frequency clock noise is known to include:
- environmental coupling,
- technical drifts,
- unknown instrumental correlations.

**Implication:**
- Failure to fully characterize these sources may produce false positives.
- Strict null tests and independent baselines are mandatory.

---

## 4. Limited Predictive Power Outside the Tested Regime

TCT v1.0 makes **one primary prediction**.
Extensions to:
- cosmology,
- quantum foundations,
- phase taxonomy,
are not required for falsification and are not independently validated.

**Implication:**
- Claims beyond the primary test are provisional.

---

## 5. Classical Limit Ambiguity

The emergence of General Relativity from stationary configurations of 𝓒
is demonstrated at a phenomenological level only.

**Implication:**
- A full derivation of Einstein equations from the coherence action
remains an open problem.

---

## 6. Quantum Interpretation Not Fixed

The quantum nature of 𝓒 (if any) is unspecified:
- no quantization scheme is defined,
- no particle interpretation is assumed.

**Implication:**
- TCT does not currently address quantum gravity directly.

---

## 7. Potential Redundancy with Existing Frameworks

It remains possible that TCT:
- reformulates known physics in alternative variables, or
- captures known correlations under a new interpretation.

**Implication:**
- Independent experimental discrimination is essential.

---

## 8. Risk of Non-Falsifiability via Parameter Adjustment

Although a falsification criterion is stated,
future extensions could improperly weaken it.

**Mitigation:**
- The core prediction in EXPERIMENT.md is fixed.
- Parameter tuning beyond stated bounds is disallowed.

---

## 9. Data Availability Constraints

Existing public clock datasets may be insufficient
to conclusively test the predicted correlation strength.

**Implication:**
- Dedicated experiments may be required.
- Null results from current data remain informative.

---

## 10. Human Interpretation Bias

Given the conceptual novelty of coherence-based time,
there is a risk of overinterpretation or metaphysical extrapolation.

**Mitigation:**
- Interpretive layers are explicitly excluded from the core model.
- Only operational claims are considered valid.

---

## Status

This list is intentionally incomplete.
New limitations discovered through analysis or experiment
must be appended, not removed.

TCT remains viable **only insofar as these limitations are respected**.
